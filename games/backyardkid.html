About the game

Finn is grounded, but the fun fair is right behind the backyard! Move with the stick and sneak through the house while Mom cooks, Dad watches the game and Grandma knits, and grab your sneakers, ticket and backpack. Wait until they look away, hide in wardrobes and boxes, throw squeaky toys to distract them, ring the phone to lure them out of the room and never step on a squeaky duck. Get caught and it is a funny trip back to your room, so be extra sneaky and slip out the window in 100 levels in 5 worlds.

  • 100 levels in 5 worlds: sunny cottage, beach house, cookie ranch, jelly villa and moon mansion
  • Hide, tiptoe, throw toys, ring gadgets and find keys to sneak past the whole family
  • Funny family reactions, a big 3D fun fair finale and 6 kid outfits
  • Real 3D worlds with silly heroes and lots of content
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
