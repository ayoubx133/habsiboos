About the game

Hold your finger on the screen to roll and let go to slow down, then drag left and right to steer your goo ball along long twisting tracks high above the clouds. Jump off spring ramps, balance on thin beams, wait for the swinging foam hammers, ride wobbly jelly rafts and boing on moon bounce pads, and if you tumble off, you bounce right back to the last checkpoint. Cross the finish gate fast to roll onto the big bonus pads, collect coins and gems and unlock 6 funny goo ball skins. Play 100 levels in 5 worlds, with a giant silly snack ball chase on every 10th level.

  • 100 levels in 5 worlds with a new track trick in every world
  • Simple controls: hold to roll, drag to steer
  • 6 funny goo ball skins and a giant snack ball chase every 10th level
  • Real 3D worlds with silly heroes and lots of content
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
