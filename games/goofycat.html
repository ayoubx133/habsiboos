About the game

You are a silly goo kitty with a stretchy paw and every room is full of things to knock over. Move with the stick, tap SWAT to knock cups, plates and books off the counters, roll yarn balls into everything, ring the bells and POUNCE onto cakes and comfy laptops for a nap. Chain your mess for big combos and ZOOMIES, finish the mess list and fill the mess meter to earn three stars before the clock runs out, while Granny Pip chases you with her tickly feather duster. Make a mess in 100 rooms in 5 worlds: the kitchen, a bakery, a toy shop, a museum with a robo vacuum to ride and a birthday party full of balloons.

  • 100 rooms in 5 worlds: kitchen, bakery, toy shop, museum and birthday party
  • Swat, pounce, roll yarn, ring bells, pop balloons and nap on the laptop
  • Funny Granny chases, big combos, ZOOMIES and 6 silly kitty skins
  • Real 3D worlds with silly heroes and lots of content
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
