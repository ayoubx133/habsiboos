About the game

Explore a giant 3D block world through the eyes of Gooby, a happy orange goo blob: move with the stick, drag to look around, hold on a block to mine it and tap to build. Chop wood, craft a pickaxe, axe and shovel at your craft table, bake glass and cookies in the oven, build a shelter with a door and a cozy bed, and tame puppies, kitties, ponies and piggies with their favourite snacks. When night falls, silly goblins sneak in to snatch your blocks, so light torches and tickle them until they giggle and give everything back. Finish 60 quests and 3 daily tasks across 5 lands (Sunny Snack Hills, Teal Toy Bay, Cookie Canyon, Jelly Volcano and Moon Candy Town), or switch to Creative Mode to build with every block and fly.

  • First person block world with 5 lands, 60 quests and daily tasks
  • Craft pickaxes, axes and shovels, build houses, farm wheat and bake snacks
  • Tame cute animals, day and night, giggling goblins and a Creative Mode
  • Real 3D worlds with silly heroes and lots of content
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
