Bonk wobbly rivals into the pudding pool with pillows and foam swords!

About the game

Tap one big button to hop and swing your pillow, foam sword or bubble wand at a floppy rival. Every bonk makes them wobble more, so keep bonking until they splash into the pudding pool. Play 100 levels in 5 worlds with tipping toy rafts, moving cookies, bouncy jelly and floaty moon gravity, plus a giant boss every 10th duel. Duel the computer or a friend on the same tablet, earn coins and unlock 6 silly heroes.

  • 100 levels in 5 worlds with a giant boss every 10th duel
  • One tap controls, 12 soft toy weapons and wobbly 3D physics
  • 1 player vs computer or 2 players on the same tablet
  • Real 3D worlds with silly heroes and lots of content
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
