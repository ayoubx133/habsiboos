Tap on the beat and help a squishy jelly cube hop through 100 musical 3D levels!

About the game

Jelly, a squishy orange jelly cube with a big happy face, runs by itself through colorful 3D snack worlds, and every gumdrop spike, goo pit and cookie saw sits right on a beat of the song. Tap to jump in time with the music, bounce on jump pads, tap inside jump rings, flip upside down through portals and hold to fly a cute dragon through tunnels. Crash and Jelly squishes like a pancake, then hops back to the last checkpoint, and practice mode lets you try again with no hearts lost while the progress bar shows how far you got. Play 100 levels in 5 worlds, each level with its own brand new song, bounce on DJ Ribbit the giggly frog every 10th level, collect golden jelly beans and unlock 6 jelly flavors.

  • 100 levels in 5 worlds, every level with its own song
  • Jump pads, jump rings, gravity flips, dragon rides and speed portals
  • Practice mode, checkpoints and a goofy frog DJ every 10th level
  • Real 3D worlds with silly heroes and lots of content
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
