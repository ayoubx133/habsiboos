Sneak past goofy goblin grandparents, fix the toy train and escape!

About the game

You are stuck in the big old house of two silly goblin grandparents, and you have 5 days to get out. Walk with the stick, look around by dragging, and tap TAKE to grab train wheels, chimneys, keys and toy hammers. Fix the toy train in the basement or unlock the front door, but tiptoe past Granny Gobble with her broom, napping Grumpa and Fluffy the giant pet, and hide in wardrobes or under beds when you are spotted. 100 levels in 5 worlds, from Cookie Cottage to Moonlight Manor, with burpy plants, cookie throwing and a bubble wand that makes goblins float.

  • 100 levels in 5 worlds with a special Train Day every 10th level
  • Sneak, hide, throw cookies and float goblins with a bubble wand
  • Get caught and you are just tickled back to bed for a new day
  • Real 3D worlds with silly heroes and lots of content
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
