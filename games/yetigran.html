About the game

Wake up in Granny Yeti's cozy snowy cottage and sneak out in 5 days. Walk with the stick, drag to look around, and find keys, the hammer, the secret code note and hot cocoa to open every lock on the front door. Granny hears every noise and shuffles over with her knitting needles, so tiptoe, hide in wardrobes and under beds, and toss cookies to distract her. If she catches you, she just tucks you into bed for a new day, across 100 levels in 5 worlds.

  • 100 levels in 5 worlds, with a sled escape every 10th level
  • Silly, warm Granny Yeti: caught means tucked into bed, never scary
  • Hide, tiptoe, feed the snow pup and pick sleepy, normal or zoomy granny
  • Real 3D worlds with silly heroes and lots of content
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
