About the game

Tap groups of two or more matching goo blobs to pop them on a chunky 3D board. Pop big groups to make rockets, goo bombs and rainbow goo, then tap boosters that touch for giant combos. Clear toy crates, pink jelly, licorice chains, rock candy and star ice, dig King Wobbles down to his pudding bath and burp-battle a goofy boss every 10th level. Every win gives coins to rebuild the wobbly toy kingdom room by room, with 100 levels in 5 worlds.

  • 100 levels in 5 worlds with a boss every 10th level
  • Rockets, goo bombs, rainbow goo and giant combos
  • Rebuild 5 rooms of a wobbly toy kingdom and unlock 6 kings
  • Real 3D worlds with silly heroes and lots of content
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
