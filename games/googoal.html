About the game

Lead your own monster soccer club in big, bright 3D matches, 7 against 7. Move with the stick, tap SHOOT to score, PASS to a friend, hold SPRINT to dash and poke the ball away with STEAL, no tackles and no fouls. Win coins to sign new monster players, train your team, build a bigger stadium full of cheering fans and design your own kit and badge. Play 100 matches in 5 leagues with cup finals against giant goalies, bouncy jelly bumpers, power cookies, jump pads and moon gravity.

  • 100 matches in 5 worlds with cup finals and seasons
  • Sign, train and dress your monster team
  • Grow your stadium from backyard pitch to Boo Dome
  • Real 3D worlds with silly heroes and lots of content
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
