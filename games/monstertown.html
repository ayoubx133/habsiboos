About the game

Create your own avatar with hair styles, colors, hats and outfits, then step into a sunny 3D town with a cozy home, a bunny cafe, a goo salon, a monster school, a park and a snack market. Drag friends, chef bunnies and monster pets anywhere, tap everything to see it react, cook pancakes and pizza with the bunnies and feed your friends for silly reactions like hot pepper puffs and brain freeze shivers. Decorate every room, bubble wash monsters for new colors and find 18 hidden surprises for your sticker book. Follow 65 story quests in 7 chapters plus 3 new daily tasks, and your town is saved just the way you left it.
