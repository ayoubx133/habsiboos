Run, jump and glide with Boo Bear through 100 star missions in 5 snack lands!

About the game

Run with the stick, tap A to jump and tap again to double jump, hold A to glide on a leaf and tap B to spin or ground pound. Climb sunny terraces, hop floating snack blocks, ride toy boats, bounce on jelly pads and float up moon bubbles to grab each golden star. Poof silly goo blobs, find lost chicks for Mama Chick, feed hungry Grugru, race Zippy the sparrow, crack open secret rooms and bop a big boss every 10th level. 100 levels in 5 worlds with 6 cube bear skins to unlock.

  • 100 levels in 5 worlds: snack hills, toy bay, cookie canyon, jelly volcano, moon candy town
  • Double jump, leaf glide, ground pound, jelly bounce and moon bubbles
  • Silly villagers, secret rooms, boss battles and 6 bear skins
  • Real 3D worlds with silly heroes and lots of content
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
