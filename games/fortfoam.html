About the game

Jump out of the jelly blimp, open your party umbrella and glide down onto a goofy snack island with 11 silly rivals. Grab toy foam blasters and bubble shields, tap BUILD for instant foam walls, ramps and floors, and splat everyone with foam darts while the giggly tickle fog shrinks the island. Splatted guests pop into goo and cheer from the floating stands, and the last one standing does a victory dance! Play 100 matches in 5 islands with jelly pads, supply balloons, goo geysers, moon gravity and a giant party crasher every 10th match, then unlock 30 outfits in the locker.

  • 100 matches in 5 islands with new tricks on every island
  • Build foam walls, ramps and floors in one tap
  • Locker with 30 funny outfits and toy back blings
  • Real 3D worlds with silly heroes and lots of content
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
