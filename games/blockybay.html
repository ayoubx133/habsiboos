Coming soon to the Amazon Appstore for Fire tablets

Build block houses, piers and rails on a sunny bay and watch friends move in!

About the game

Walk your squishy jelly cube around a sunny bay and tap the glowing ghost blocks to build houses, piers, boats, rail tracks, farms and castles. Finish 100 build quests on 5 islands: clean up the harbor, ride the minecart, plant and pick crops, feed the animals and light up a candy town at night. Cute villagers, chicks, bunnies and sparrows move into everything you build and cheer for you. Play free in Creative mode with every block and flying, or start on one magic block in the sky that comes back as a new block every time you break it.

  • 100 build quests on 5 sunny islands, plus daily tasks
  • Creative mode with flying and One Block sky challenge
  • Cute animals and villagers move in, 6 fun skins to unlock
  • Real 3D worlds with silly heroes and lots of content
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
