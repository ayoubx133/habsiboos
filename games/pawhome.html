Be a silly kitty in a big family house: knock things over, steal snacks, run!

About the game

Move your fluffy kitty with the joystick, JUMP onto tables, shelves and the fridge, PAW to knock cups and books off the edge and MEOW to wake up sleepy grandpa. Every day brings 3 tasks on your quest card: steal the fish, play with the kids, beg grandma for a treat, ride the robot vacuum, chase the red dot or tiptoe past the sleeping family at night. Prank grumpy grandpa and run when he chases you with his rolled newspaper, or hide up high and in boxes! Play 100 levels in 5 worlds of cozy houses, finish daily tasks and upgrade your cat bed, cat tree and toys.

  • 100 days in 5 houses with 65 different quests, plus daily tasks
  • Prank grumpy grandpa, play with the kids, ride the robot vacuum
  • Upgrade your cat bed and toys, unlock 6 cute kitty skins
  • Real 3D worlds with silly heroes and lots of content
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
