Coming soon to the Amazon Appstore for Fire tablets

Swap chunky 3D snacks, save the silly king and build a cookie palace!

About the game

Swipe a snack onto its neighbour to line up 3 or more donuts, cookies, jelly cubes and candies. Match 4 for a candy rocket, a square for a cookie propeller, an L for a popcorn bomb and 5 for a rainbow cake, then swap two boosters together for giant combos. Clear cake boxes, frosty ice, sticky honey, licorice cages and sneaky chocolate before your moves run out, and every 10th level rescue King Mushy from his goo jar. Each level you win adds a new piece to Queen Rosie's cookie palace across 100 levels in 5 worlds.

  • 100 levels in 5 worlds with a new snack challenge in each world
  • Save the King levels where silly goo rises with every move
  • Build a giant 3D cookie palace and unlock 6 royal queens
  • Real 3D worlds with silly heroes and lots of content
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
