Build your own stories in a 3D toy town: dress up, cook, play and explore!

About the game

Drag Milo, Boo the goo blob and lots of silly friends between 10 toy places: a cozy home, a bun bakery, a goo salon, a paw vet, a blob school, a snack park, a jelly market, a toy beach, a cocoa cafe and a mushroom castle. Every object reacts when you tap it: open the fridge, bake cakes in the oven, blend juice, slide, swing, surf and launch friends from the confetti cannon. Feed your friends for funny reactions like hot pepper puffs, brain freeze shivers and giant potion growth, dress them up with hats, glasses and colors, and find 20 hidden surprises. Follow 60 story quests in 10 chapters plus new daily tasks, with no rules and no timer.

  • 10 toy places to explore with lots of funny friends
  • 60 story quests, daily tasks and 20 hidden surprises
  • Dress up, cook, bake and play pretend your way
  • Real 3D worlds with silly heroes and lots of content
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
