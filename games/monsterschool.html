Monster School Days 3D

FamilyFreeOfflineNo ads

Coming soon to the Amazon Appstore for Fire tablets

Go to a silly monster school, make friends, shop in town and play at the beach!

About the game

Walk your student around a big 3D school and town with the joystick, talk to monster classmates and tap ACT to help them, buy snacks, give gifts and take photos. Classes are fun mini games: pop the right math balloon, copy the paint colours, drum to the beat, mix potions and cook without adding socks. Finish 100 story quests in 5 chapters, from the first school day to the beach, the fun park and the moon night fair, plus 3 new daily tasks every day. Dance, wave, laugh, sit and flip with your friends, ride the teacups and the ferris wheel, bounce on the trampoline and collect cute hats and 6 characters.

  • 100 story quests in 5 chapters plus daily tasks
  • Classes as mini games: math, art, music, science, cooking
  • Monster friends, shops, beach, fun park, hats and 6 skins
  • Real 3D worlds with silly heroes and lots of content
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
