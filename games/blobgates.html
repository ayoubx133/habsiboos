About the game

Drag to steer your King and his army of squishy Boo blobs down the castle road. Walk through the blue gates to grow your army with +5 and x2, dodge the red ones, and let your blobs throw tickle bubbles to free trapped friends from barrels and giggle rival squads away. At the end your whole army bounces over the castle wall and tickles every guard until the flag turns blue! Play 100 levels in 5 worlds with rolling pins, swinging foam hammers, goo catapults and a silly giant lord every 10th level, then upgrade your army and unlock 6 royal heroes.

  • Upgrade army, tickle power and loot, unlock 6 royal heroes
  • Real 3D worlds with silly heroes and lots of content
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
